What is TSA?

The Technology Student Association (TSA) is a national organization dedicated to students engaged in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM). We build leadership, teamwork, and technical skills through competitive events and community projects.

TSA Dress Code

Professionalism starts with how you present yourself. Hover over the cards below to see what is required at official TSA conferences.

✅ OFFICIAL ATTIRE

Hover to reveal requirements

  • 👔 Shirt: Official TSA royal blue button-down
  • 👖 Bottoms: Light gray pants or skirt
  • 👞 Shoes: Black dress shoes with black socks
  • 🧥 Blazer: Navy blue with TSA patch (optional but encouraged)
  • 🎀 Accessories: Official TSA tie or ascot

🚫 NOT ALLOWED

Hover to reveal restrictions

  • 👖 Denim: No jeans or denim of any color
  • 🩳 Casual: No shorts, sweatpants, or leggings
  • 👟 Footwear: No sneakers, sandals, or crocs
  • 👕 Tops: No t-shirts or athletic wear
  • 🧢 Accessories: No hats or sunglasses indoors
